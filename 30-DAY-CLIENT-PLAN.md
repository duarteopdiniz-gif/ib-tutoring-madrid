## THE MATH FIRST — how many conversations = 4 clients/week

Typical tutoring funnel conversion (cold-ish outreach):
- Outreach → reply: ~20–30%
- Reply → intro call booked: ~40%
- Intro call → trial/first lesson: ~60%
- Trial → paying client: ~60%

**Net: roughly 3–5% of cold outreaches become clients.**

To get **4 clients/week**, aim for:
- **~90–120 quality outreaches/week** (≈ 15–20/day, 6 days)
- → ~25 replies → ~10 intro calls → ~6 trials → ~4 clients

Warm channels (referrals, school lists) convert far higher (15–30%), so as those grow,
you need less cold volume. **Weeks 1–2 are cold-heavy; weeks 3–4 shift to warm.**

## THE 30-DAY PLAN (4 weeks)

### WEEK 1 — Foundation + go live on high-intent channels
- Day 1: Create profiles on **Tusclasesparticulares, Superprof, Preply**. Link website. Set area = "central Madrid + online".
- Day 2: Join 6–8 Facebook expat/parent groups. Read rules. Post intro in 2 (see scripts).
- Day 3: Email **ICS** (alumni tutor list) + 3 Tier-1 schools. Post in 2 more FB groups.
- Day 4: Set up **Instagram @ibtutoringmadrid** + LinkedIn. Message 5 IE friends for referrals.
- Day 5: Follow up all replies within 2 hrs. Post 1st Instagram tip.
- Day 6: Visit 5 central cafés/libraries — leave QR cards where allowed.
- **Target W1:** 90 outreaches, 5+ intro calls, 2–3 clients.

### WEEK 2 — Volume + first trials
- Daily: 15–20 outreaches (mix FB comments, marketplace replies, DMs).
- Book + run intro calls. Convert to trial lessons. Deliver a great first lesson.
- Ask every family: "Do you know another family who might want this?" (referral ask).
- **Target W2:** 100 outreaches, 4 clients, 3 referral asks made.

### WEEK 3 — Shift to warm + referrals
- Follow up school/PTA contacts. Attend 1 expat meetup.
- Post 3 Instagram tips. Ask satisfied clients for a 1-line testimonial (add to site).
- Launch **referral offer**: "Refer a family, you both get a free 30 min."
- **Target W3:** 70 cold + 20 warm outreaches, 4–5 clients.

### WEEK 4 — Optimise + systematise
- Review KPIs (below). Double down on the 1–2 channels converting best; drop the worst.
- Ask for reviews on your best marketplace profile (ranking boost).
- Decide on price/positioning tweaks (see "raise prices" section).
- **Target W4:** 4–6 clients, funnel running on referrals + top 2 channels.

## HOW TO POSITION DIFFERENTLY FROM GENERIC TUTORS

- **"I did the IB myself, here, recently."** Insider, not a generic adult tutor.
- **Bilingual** — bridges international AND Spanish families.
- **Focus on Grades 1–10 habits**, not just cramming — parents value long-term.
- **You're a connector for DP too** — you solve the whole family's needs, even beyond your subjects.
- **Professional bilingual website + fast replies** — most local tutors have neither.

## SIMPLE CRM / FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM (free)

Use a **Google Sheet** with columns:
`Name | Child grade | Channel | Subject | Status | Last contact | Next action | Notes`

Statuses: `New → Replied → Call booked → Trial → Client → Lost`

**Follow-up rules (never lose a lead):**
- No reply in 2 days → 1 friendly nudge.
- After intro call → same-day message with a proposed trial time.
- After trial → next-day "shall we book a regular slot?" message.
- Lost/cold → re-touch after 3–4 weeks ("still happy to help if useful").

## MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Slow replies (biggest killer — parents book whoever answers first).
- Spamming groups → bans. Post once, be helpful, let the link work.
- Only doing cold outreach; not engineering referrals from day 1.
- No tracking — you won't know what's working.
- Vague messaging ("I tutor everything"). Be specific: Grades 1–10, subjects, area.
- Underpricing forever (see below).

---

## LOW-BUDGET GROWTH (< €150/month)

- QR business cards: ~€15 one-off
- 1 marketplace premium boost (optional): ~€20–40/mo
- Local Instagram post boost, tightly targeted to central Madrid parents: ~€30–50/mo
- Coffee at 1 meetup/week: ~€20/mo
- **Keep the rest for referral rewards (free lessons).** Referrals beat paid ads for tutoring.

---

## SHOULD YOU RAISE PRICES? WHEN?

- Your €15/€20 is intentionally low to win first clients fast — good for launch.
- **Raise once you hit ~80% booked / a waitlist** (likely after week 3–4 if this works).
- Step up gradually: €20→€25 online, €25→€30 in-person, for NEW clients first.
- Keep loyal early clients at their rate for a while (goodwill + referrals).
