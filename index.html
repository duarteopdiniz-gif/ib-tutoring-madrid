About Me

A tutor who knows Madrid's international schools

Hi, I'm Duarte. I graduated from the IB Diploma Programme at the International College Spain (ICS) and I'm now a student at IE University in Madrid. I grew up in this exact school system, so I understand what international students are going through — and I speak both English and Spanish, which makes it easy to work with international and Spanish families alike.

My focus is on younger students — Grades 1 through 10 (Primary and the IB Middle Years Programme). This is where good habits are built. I've tutored with IB Bridge, and I help students understand their subjects, get organised, and grow the confidence that sets them up for the tougher years ahead.

And when a family needs specialist help for the IB Diploma (DP) — the hardest final years — I connect them with expert tutors studying those exact subjects at university. More on that below.

Services

How I can help your child

My main focus is supporting students in Grades 1–10. For the IB Diploma years, I offer a specialist service with expert subject tutors.

📗

MYP & Primary Tutoring (Grades 1–10)

My core service. Friendly, patient one-to-one help across school subjects for younger students — Physical Sciences, Design, Humanities and general schoolwork — building understanding at a pace that suits your child.

📚

Study & Organisation Skills

Many capable students struggle not with content, but with staying organised. I help your child build study routines, plan homework and deadlines, and revise effectively — habits that lower stress and lift grades.

🎓

IB Diploma (DP) Specialist Tutoring

For the demanding final IB years, I connect your child with specialist online tutors who are currently studying those exact subjects at university degree level — so they get expert, up-to-date help in Physics, Maths, Economics and more.
