About Me

A tutor who has actually done the IB

Hi, I'm Duarte. I graduated from the IB Diploma Programme at the International College Spain (ICS) and I'm now a student at IE University in Madrid. I know exactly how demanding the Diploma can feel — the deadlines, the internal assessments, the balancing act between six subjects, the Extended Essay and TOK.

During my own diploma I specialised in Physics, Mathematics: Analysis & Approaches (AA), Economics, Geography, and English Language & Literature. I've since spent time tutoring with IB Bridge, helping students build the understanding and study systems that make the difference between struggling and succeeding.

My approach is simple: I don't just help your child pass a test. I help them understand the material, get organised, and feel confident — skills that carry them through the entire diploma and beyond.

Services

How I can help your child

Whether your child is just starting the diploma or needs targeted help before an exam, I offer three clear ways to support them.

🎓

Get Ready for the IB Diploma

Starting the DP soon? I help students understand the structure of the IB Diploma Programme — subject groups, HL vs SL, the core (TOK, Extended Essay, CAS), assessments and grading — so they begin with confidence instead of confusion.

📚

Overall Study & Organisation Skills

Many capable students struggle not with the content, but with staying organised. I help your child build study routines, plan deadlines, manage internal assessments and revise effectively — the habits that lower stress and lift grades.

🎯

Subject-Specific Support

Is your child finding a particular subject difficult? I provide focused one-to-one help in the subjects I specialise in — breaking down tricky topics until they truly make sense.
