# IB Tutoring Madrid — Duarte Oliveira

A professional one-page website for private IB tutoring in Madrid, targeting international families.

**Live site:** https://duarteopdiniz-gif.github.io/ib-tutoring-madrid/

## About
- IB Diploma graduate (ICS), student at IE University
- Tutoring across **IB Diploma Programme (DP)** and **Middle Years Programme (MYP)**
- Online (worldwide) and in-person (Madrid)

### Subjects
- **DP:** Physics · Maths AA · Economics · Geography · English Language & Literature · DP structure & study skills
- **MYP:** Physical Sciences · Design · Humanities

## Adding your photo
Save your headshot as **`photo.jpg`** in this folder (same place as `index.html`) and it will
appear automatically in the hero card. Until then a stylised "DO" placeholder is shown.

## Files
- `index.html` — page content
- `styles.css` — styling
