# Marketing Plan — IB Tutoring Madrid (Duarte Oliveira)

**Website:** https://duarteopdiniz-gif.github.io/ib-tutoring-madrid/

**Goal:** Get first paying students — focus on younger students (Grades 1–10 / Primary & MYP),
with the €25 DP specialist service as an upsell.

**Your advantages:** IB graduate & insider, English + Spanish, based centrally in Madrid,
professional website already live.

**Constraints:** Schools won't allow posters. Prefer in-person clients in **central Madrid**
(easy to reach), plus online clients anywhere.

---

## 1. Your #1 tool: the website link

Every single message, post or profile below should end with your link:
👉 **https://duarteopdiniz-gif.github.io/ib-tutoring-madrid/**

It makes you look far more serious than tutors who just drop a phone number.

---

## 2. Where international & Spanish families actually are (online)

These replace the WhatsApp-group approach that didn't work.

### A. Expat & international-parent Facebook groups (highest priority)
Search and join these, then post a short, friendly intro (see template below):
- "Expats in Madrid"
- "Americans in Madrid"
- "British in Madrid" / "Brits in Madrid"
- "Madrid Mums" / "Mums in Madrid"
- "International Families Madrid"
- "Madrid Expat Community"

**Rules to not get banned:** read group rules first; don't spam; post once, be helpful,
answer questions people ask about school/tutoring, and let your link do the work.

### B. InterNations Madrid (internations.org)
Huge expat network. Create a free profile, join Madrid events, mention what you do naturally.

### C. Tutoring marketplaces (parents actively search here)
Create profiles — these send you clients without you chasing:
- **Superprof** (very popular in Spain)
- **Tusclasesparticulares.com** (big in Spain, Spanish + international families)
- **Preply** / **Tutorful** (more online-focused)
Put your website link in each profile.

### D. Your IE University network
- Post in IE student WhatsApp/Telegram groups — many classmates have younger siblings
  or babysit/tutor for expat families and can refer you.
- IE has a large international community; word of mouth here is gold.

---

## 3. Focusing on CENTRAL Madrid (in-person)

Since you want easy-access, central clients:

- **Set a service area** on your marketplace profiles: "In-person in central Madrid
  (Chamberí, Salamanca, Centro, Retiro, Chamartín) — or online anywhere."
- **Target central-ish international schools' catchment** — families of these schools often
  live centrally and search online:
  - Hastings School, International College Spain (your alma mater!), King's College,
    British Council School, ESMS, Kensington School.
- **Public library notice areas & cafés** with community boards in central barrios
  (Chamberí, Malasaña, Salamanca) — a small professional flyer/QR card where allowed.
- **Local WhatsApp/Telegram neighbourhood groups** for central barrios.

---

## 4. Leverage your old school (ICS) — the poster ban workaround

Schools won't let you poster, but they *will* often:
- Let alumni be listed in an **alumni tutor directory** or newsletter — ask ICS directly.
- Pass your info to their **parent association / PTA** if you ask nicely (you're a graduate).
- Let you attend alumni events where you meet current parents.
Email the school: short, polite, "ICS graduate now at IE offering tutoring, may I be listed
for parents seeking tutors?"

## 5. Instagram — a simple, credible presence

- Create **@ibtutoringmadrid** (or similar). Bio = one line + your website link.
- Post weekly, low effort: one IB/study tip, one "how the MYP works" explainer,
  one testimonial (once you get them). Reels do well.
- Follow & interact with Madrid expat/parenting accounts.

---

## 6. Referrals & first-client tactics

- **Offer a free 20-min intro call** (already on your site) — low pressure, high conversion.
- **First-lesson discount** for new families (e.g. first session €10) to reduce risk.
- **Referral reward:** "Refer a family, get a free hour." Parents talk to other parents.
- Ask happy families for a **1-line testimonial** — I can add these to your website.

---

## 7. Simple weekly routine (30–45 min/day)

- **Mon:** Post/answer in 1–2 Facebook expat groups.
- **Tue:** Update/check marketplace profiles (Superprof, Tusclases…).
- **Wed:** 1 Instagram post + reply to comments/DMs.
- **Thu:** Reach out to 2–3 contacts (IE friends, ICS, neighbourhood groups).
- **Fri:** Follow up with anyone who enquired; reply within a few hours always.

Consistency beats intensity. A little every day compounds fast.

---

## 8. Track what works

Keep a simple note: where did each enquiry come from? After 3–4 weeks you'll see which
1–2 channels bring the most families — then double down on those.
